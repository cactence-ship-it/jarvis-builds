# Engineering Decisions

Each decision is recorded once. Revise by adding a new decision that supersedes the old
one; never edit history. Status: ACTIVE | SUPERSEDED.

| ID | Decision | Rationale | Status | Date |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-001 | Primary CAD is Python-driven parametric modelling with **build123d 0.13.0** on **OpenCascade** (`cadquery-ocp-novtk` 8.0.1.1). | Code-defined geometry is editable, reviewable and reproducible in a temporary container, can be driven entirely from chat, and exports STEP natively. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-01 |
| D-002 | **GitHub is the persistent source of truth.** Work happens on a feature branch with a draft PR. Every session ends with `scripts/checkpoint.sh`, which reports VERIFIED only when the GitHub branch head equals local HEAD. | The container is wiped when a session ends. Persistence is proven by test (E-005), not assumed. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-01 |
| D-003 | **Vehicle Coordinate System (VCS):** right-handed; X longitudinal, positive rearward; Y lateral, positive to the right; Z vertical, positive up; origin X=0 at the front wheel centre line, Y=0 at the centre plane, Z=0 at the ground at the reference state. LH = -Y. **glTF export:** glTF (x, y, z) = VCS (-Y, Z, -X) x 0.001 m, so the vehicle front faces glTF +Z and up is +Y. | Matches common OEM body coordinates (most of the car has positive X), is easy to measure from the ground with a tape, and maps by a proper rotation (det = +1, tested) to the glTF convention used by Android viewers. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-01 |
| D-004 | Units: mm, kg, deg, cm3, kW, N·m, L, rpm, V. Tolerances are stated in the parameter's own unit. | One unit system; no silent conversions. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-01 |
| D-005 | **Evidence rules, enforced by code** (`tools/twin.py validate`, tests): UNKNOWN means a null value. ESTIMATED needs a source and a tolerance greater than 0. VERIFIED needs confidence HIGH plus a RECEIVED photo, document or measurement. Owner statements alone are ESTIMATED at most. Unregistered evidence IDs are rejected. | Makes fabrication and silent promotion of estimates mechanically impossible to commit. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-01 |
| D-006 | Three separate representations per component: **visual**, **cad** (engineering) and **mechanical** (interfaces and motion), each with its own geometry status in `assembly/vehicle_master.yaml`. | Prevents a nice-looking mesh being mistaken for engineering geometry. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-01 |
| D-007 | **Minimal dependencies:** 2 direct packages (build123d, pyyaml), 59 hash-pinned in `requirements.lock`, installed into `.venv` by the idempotent `scripts/setup_env.sh` (uv, with a pip fallback). Blender, Open3D, PyVista, Gmsh, trimesh and similar are added only when a specific task needs them, with the reason recorded in `requirements.in`. Tests use stdlib `unittest`. | Fast, reproducible sessions (15 s fresh install, about 3 s when up to date) and a small attack and failure surface. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-01 |
| D-008 | **Reference preservation:** originals are only ever written by `tools/ingest_refs.py`. The copy is byte-identical and SHA-256 verified, read-only, never overwritten, and recorded in an append-only `refs/MANIFEST.csv`. `.gitattributes` disables end-of-line conversion. Analysis works on `refs/derived/`. | The source evidence must never be altered or lost. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-01 |
| D-009 | The project lives in its own folder `kartal-s-twin/` inside the multi-project `jarvis-builds` repository. | Avoids interfering with other builds. The existing README.md and tag are untouched apart from one appended pointer line. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-01 |
| D-010 | Generated outputs (`build/`: STEP, STL, GLB, renders) are not committed. They are rebuilt from code and delivered as ZIP or GLB files on request. Small release packages may later be attached to GitHub Releases. | Keeps the repository small and avoids stale binaries that drift from the source. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-01 |
| D-011 | Exports are accepted only after an **independent re-open**: STEP through the kernel (validity, volume, bounding box), and STL and GLB through numpy-only readers (watertight, consistent winding, outward normals, scale, axes). | An exporter cannot vouch for itself. The exporter conventions were measured, not assumed (E-006). | ACTIVE | 2026-11-01 |
| D-012 | **The repository is PUBLIC** (E-005). Until the owner explicitly decides otherwise: no photos, archives, document scans, registration plate, VIN or personal data in git. `refs/original` and `refs/derived` are git-ignored, and a privacy scan (plate pattern, e-mail, media files) blocks every checkpoint. The public alias for the car is **VEH-01**. | The directive forbids publishing private photos or personal data without authorisation. Making the repository private, or choosing a private store, is the owner's decision (U-009). | ACTIVE | 2026-11-01 |
| D-013 | **Placeholder geometry is allowed only through `specs/placeholder_assumptions.yaml`** and only for the visual representation. Builders read every value through `kartal_twin.params.Resolver`, which records provenance. Geometry status is derived from it: any placeholder gives PLACEHOLDER, ESTIMATED or VERIFIED inputs give DRAFT, and VALIDATED is never automatic. A spec value that is not UNKNOWN always overrides the placeholder. | Lets the end-to-end pipeline (build, export, validation, Android viewing) run before evidence arrives, without any guess being mistaken for data. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-02 |
| D-014 | The GLB for Android is written by our own writer (`kartal_twin.gltf_writer`): one node per component (name = component ID, extras = status and parameter provenance), grouped per assembly under VEHICLE_MASTER, per-face smooth normals, in metres with the D-003 axes. STEP uses build123d/OCCT with labelled, coloured solids. | Full control over metadata for component inspection in the viewer; the OCCT glTF writer does not carry our extras. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-02 |
