| D-003 | **Vehicle Coordinate System (VCS):** right-handed; X longitudinal, positive rearward; Y lateral, positive to the right; Z vertical, positive up; origin X=0 at the front wheel centre line, Y=0 at the centre plane, Z=0 at the ground at the reference state.
