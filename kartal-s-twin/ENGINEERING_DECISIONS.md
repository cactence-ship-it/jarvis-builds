# Engineering Decisions

Each decision is recorded once. Revise by adding a new decision that supersedes the old
one; never edit history. Status: ACTIVE | SUPERSEDED.

| ID | Decision | Rationale | Status | Date |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-001 | Primary CAD is Python-driven parametric modelling with **build123d 0.13.0** on **OpenCascade** (`cadquery-ocp-novtk` 8.0.1.1). | Code-defined geometry is editable, reviewable and reproducible in a temporary container, can be driven entirely from chat, and exports STEP natively. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-01 |
| D-002 | **GitHub is the persistent source of truth.** Work happens on a feature branch with a draft PR. Every session ends with `scripts/checkpoint.sh`, which reports VERIFIED only when the GitHub branch head equals local HEAD. | The container is wiped when a session ends. Persistence is proven by test (E-005), not assumed. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-01 |
| D-003 | **Vehicle Coordinate System (VCS):** right-handed; X longitudinal, positive rearward; Y lateral, positive to the right; Z vertical, positive up; origin X=0 at the front wheel centre line, Y=0 at the centre plane, Z=0 at the ground at the reference state. LH = -Y. **glTF export:** glTF (x, y, z) = VCS (-Y, Z, -X) x 0.001 m, so the vehicle front faces glTF +Z and up is +Y. | Matches common OEM body coordinates (most of the car has positive X), is easy to measure from the ground with a tape, and maps by a proper rotation (det = +1, tested) to the glTF convention used by Android viewers. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-01 |
| D-004 | Units: mm, kg, deg, cm3, kW, N·m, L, rpm, V. Tolerances are stated in the parameter's own unit. | One unit system; no silent conversions. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-01 |
| D-005 | **Evidence rules, enforced by code** (`tools/twin.py validate`, tests): UNKNOWN means a null value. ESTIMATED needs a source and a tolerance greater than 0. VERIFIED needs confidence HIGH plus a RECEIVED photo, document or measurement. Owner statements alone are ESTIMATED at most. Unregistered evidence IDs are rejected. | Makes fabrication and silent promotion of estimates mechanically impossible to commit. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-01 |
| D-006 | Three separate representations per component: **visual**, **cad** (engineering) and **mechanical** (interfaces and motion), each with its own geometry status in `assembly/vehicle_master.yaml`. | Prevents a nice-looking mesh being mistaken for engineering geometry. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-01 |
| D-007 | **Minimal dependencies:** 2 direct packages (build123d, pyyaml), 59 hash-pinned in `requirements.lock`, installed into `.venv` by the idempotent `scripts/setup_env.sh` (uv, with a pip fallback). Blender, Open3D, PyVista, Gmsh, trimesh and similar are added only when a specific task needs them, with the reason recorded in `requirements.in`. Tests use stdlib `unittest`. | Fast, reproducible sessions (15 s fresh install, about 3 s when up to date) and a small attack and failure surface. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-01 |
| D-008 | **Reference preservation:** originals are only ever written by `tools/ingest_refs.py`. The copy is byte-identical and SHA-256 verified, read-only, never overwritten, and recorded in an append-only `refs/MANIFEST.csv`. `.gitattributes` disables end-of-line conversion. Analysis works on `refs/derived/`. | The source evidence must never be altered or lost. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-01 |
| D-009 | The project lives in its own folder `kartal-s-twin/` inside the multi-project `jarvis-builds` repository. | Avoids interfering with other builds. The existing README.md and tag are untouched apart from one appended pointer line. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-01 |
| D-010 | Generated outputs (`build/`: STEP, STL, GLB, renders) are not committed. They are rebuilt from code and delivered as ZIP or GLB files on request. Small release packages may later be attached to GitHub Releases. | Keeps the repository small and avoids stale binaries that drift from the source. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-01 |
| D-011 | Exports are accepted only after an **independent re-open**: STEP through the kernel (validity, volume, bounding box), and STL and GLB through numpy-only readers (watertight, consistent winding, outward normals, scale, axes). | An exporter cannot vouch for itself. The exporter conventions were measured, not assumed (E-006). | ACTIVE | 2026-11-01 |
| D-012 | **The repository is PUBLIC** (E-005). Until the owner explicitly decides otherwise: no photos, archives, document scans, registration plate, VIN or personal data in git. `refs/original` and `refs/derived` are git-ignored, and a privacy scan (plate pattern, e-mail, media files) blocks every checkpoint. The public alias for the car is **VEH-01**. | The directive forbids publishing private photos or personal data without authorisation. Making the repository private, or choosing a private store, is the owner's decision (U-009). | ACTIVE | 2026-11-01 |
| D-013 | **Placeholder geometry is allowed only through `specs/placeholder_assumptions.yaml`** and only for the visual representation. Builders read every value through `kartal_twin.params.Resolver`, which records provenance. Geometry status is derived from it: any placeholder gives PLACEHOLDER, ESTIMATED or VERIFIED inputs give DRAFT, and VALIDATED is never automatic. A spec value that is not UNKNOWN always overrides the placeholder. | Lets the end-to-end pipeline (build, export, validation, Android viewing) run before evidence arrives, without any guess being mistaken for data. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-02 |
| D-014 | The GLB for Android is written by our own writer (`kartal_twin.gltf_writer`): one node per component (name = component ID, extras = status and parameter provenance), grouped per assembly under VEHICLE_MASTER, per-face smooth normals, in metres with the D-003 axes. STEP uses build123d/OCCT with labelled, coloured solids. | Full control over metadata for component inspection in the viewer; the OCCT glTF writer does not carry our extras. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-02 |
| D-015 | **Owner tape measurements count as direct evidence.** A MEASUREMENT evidence item whose values are RECORDED in `evidence/measurements/<E-id>.yaml` (committed: values only, no photos or personal data) can support VERIFIED with confidence HIGH and an explicit tolerance. Input goes through `tools/record_measurement.py` using the codes in `docs/OLCUM_REHBERI.md`. Every shape placeholder now has a spec slot (`body_profile.*`), so a measurement replaces its placeholder automatically. A placeholder that stands in for a spec slot can no longer be read directly. | The owner has the physical car; a measured value with a stated reference state and tolerance is reliable direct evidence, and it is the fastest route from PLACEHOLDER to DRAFT geometry. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-02 |
| D-016 | **Engineering CAD uses evidence only.** A Resolver built for the `cad` or `mechanical` representation refuses every placeholder. CAD geometry from ESTIMATED inputs is DRAFT; VALIDATED needs measured evidence plus a recorded check. The first CAD parts are the four tire design envelopes (annulus OD x bead-seat diameter x section width, from the 175/70 R14 designation), in the unloaded position (wheel centre at the free radius, since static deflection has no evidence). STEP files carry a fixed header timestamp so they are byte-reproducible. | Separates engineering geometry from the visual placeholder envelope (D-006, D-013) and makes outputs auditable by SHA-256. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-02 |
| D-017 | Exception to D-010: dated audit snapshots (`audit/<date>/`) may commit small generated CAD outputs (STEP, manifest, test log, SHA256SUMS) so external reviewers can check them without running the code. Only generated geometry: never owner media or personal data. | Independent review (ChatGPT cross-check) needs the artefacts themselves, not just claims. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-02 |
| D-018 | **Interface and clearance checks** run on the engineering CAD representation with exact B-rep operations (OpenCascade distance, boolean common). Rules live in `specs/clearance_rules.yaml`; only definitional rules until evidence exists; a rule that needs missing geometry reports NOT_EVALUATED; a FAIL makes the build report an error. | The directive requires assembly interface and clearance verification; exact geometry avoids mesh-approximation disputes between reviewers. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-02 |
| D-019 | **Task registry and cross-review.** `tasks/T-nnn.yaml` is the persistent task queue (ID = file name, append-only results and reviews). A task is ACCEPTED only when an agent other than the producer reviewed the same commit with ACCEPT; enforced by `tools/twin.py validate`. Blocked tasks must name the exact blocker. | Makes "no self-approval" and "never fabricate another agent's approval" checkable; works with zero paid services; any orchestrator (n8n, Actions) can read the same files later. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-02 |
| D-020 | **Literature confidence cap.** Values supported only by aggregator pages or sale listings read through search summaries (E-007..E-013) are ESTIMATED with confidence LOW, never MEDIUM or higher, until a primary source (manufacturer document, owner's manual, ruhsat) or a measurement confirms them. Left and right wheelbases are always used per side, never averaged. | ChatGPT review of T-001 (2026-11-02): weak source traceability and variant applicability; averaging hid measured asymmetry. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-02 |
| D-021 | **Dimensional consistency rules** (`specs/consistency_rules.yaml`, `kartal_twin.consistency`): relations every correct measurement set must satisfy, evaluated with worst-case tolerance stacking; UNKNOWN inputs give NOT_EVALUATED; any FAIL fails `validate` and therefore blocks every checkpoint. The engineering CAD STEP output is additionally pinned to the latest dated audit snapshot (`tests/test_golden_cad.py`); intended geometry changes need a new snapshot. | Catches unit, code and typing errors in owner measurements before they reach geometry; makes unintended geometry drift a test failure. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-02 |
| D-022 | **Worst-case interval semantics for consistency rules** (supersedes the tolerance wording in D-021). Inequalities PASS only if they hold for every point of the input intervals, FAIL if violated for every point, otherwise INDETERMINATE (not blocking, reported for better measurement). Identities PASS when the intervals overlap. | ChatGPT review of T-013 (c665cfb): the first version could PASS inside the uncertainty band, e.g. tire radius vs arch clearance. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-02 |
| D-023 | **Record integrity.** Task results and reviews must cite real git SHAs (validator rejects any other text; a test checks every SHA exists in history; CI uses full history). Results are recorded in a commit *after* the work commit, citing its SHA. SHA-256 integrity of audit snapshots and the reference manifest is verified in `validate` (T-018). The privacy guard also covers VINs and credentials, and a whole-history audit runs in CI (T-019). | Placeholder text had been written into four result records instead of SHAs; records must be checkable. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-02 |
| D-024 | **Commit-reference semantics.** A commit reference is 7-40 lowercase hex characters; a short SHA is valid when `git rev-parse --verify <sha>^{commit}` resolves it to exactly one commit (ambiguous or unknown prefixes are errors); short and full forms are equivalent and historical records are not rewritten. Results carry `round` and `superseded_by`, so earlier rounds stay visible. CI logs every test by name (`unittest -v`) and re-checks audit snapshots with coreutils `sha256sum -c`, independent of the project's Python hashing code. | ChatGPT's batch-3 evidence requests (2026-11-02). | ACTIVE | 2026-11-02 |
| D-025 | **Registration documents verify registered values only.** A RECEIVED ruhsat photo makes a parameter VERIFIED only for the value printed in its field (e.g. net weight, cylinder volume, fuel kind). Readings that need interpretation (make 'MURAT' as Tofaş, 'İstiap haddi' as seat count) stay ESTIMATED. Empty fields verify nothing. The present physical state (mass with the LPG kit, a swapped engine) still needs measurement or photos. Plate, VIN, engine number, document serials and dates are never transcribed into the public repository. | The owner sent the registration documents (2026-11-02); they are primary sources (D-020) but describe the car as registered, and they carry identifying data. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-02 |
| D-026 | **Owner configuration, data classes, LPG and wheel CAD.** (1) Data classes MEASURED / DOCUMENT_CONFIRMED / USER_CONFIRMED / SOURCE_SUPPORTED / ESTIMATED / UNKNOWN are DERIVED from status, confidence and evidence type (`kartal_twin.spec.data_class`), never stored, so D-005 stays the single rule: an owner statement is still ESTIMATED, shown as USER_CONFIRMED. (2) New evidence type ASSUMPTION for provisional modelling values (owner-supplied or engineering): ESTIMATED with confidence LOW only, enforced by `validate`. (3) Engine variant separation: `specs/configuration_rules.yaml` forbids engine.* values from sources that describe a different engine variant than engine.fuel_system_type (carburettor excludes the 1.6 i.e. page E-009). (4) The LPG system is a separate set of FUEL_SYSTEM components (FUL-005..014) and spec group `lpg_system`. (5) CAD parts whose position has no evidence are built in their own local frame and marked unplaced: exported and checked alone, kept out of the vehicle assembly and clearance checks, whose rules then report NOT_EVALUATED (BLOCKED) with the reason. (6) Intended CAD changes get a new snapshot `audit/2026-11-02-r2`; the earlier snapshot is kept unchanged. | Owner directive 2026-11-02 (configuration, LPG tank, steel rims, provisional values allowed but labelled). | ACTIVE | 2026-11-02 |
| D-027 | **Complete-vehicle PLACEHOLDER package.** Every assembly has visual geometry (150 visual parts; 7 components excepted with a stated reason). Mechanical layout guesses live only in `specs/placeholder_assumptions.yaml` (`pkg_*`, each with a rationale) so every such part is PLACEHOLDER by construction (tested); photos fix only the arrangement. Where the TYPE is unknown (front brake disc/drum, rear spring coil/leaf, steering gear rack/ball, anti-roll bar) a neutral envelope is drawn and the note says so; LPG and wiring are schematic routes, never installation or circuit data. Engineering CAD is exported before the visual model, because OpenCascade numbers STEP assembly occurrences with a counter that ran across both exports (CAD bytes depended on the visual part count); snapshot `audit/2026-11-02-r4` has the same geometry as r3. | Owner master directive 2026-11-03: a complete inspectable 3D twin without fabricated measurements. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-03 |
| D-028 | **Realistic visual body (Blender), visual only.** A second visual representation is modelled in Blender (`tools/blender/kartal_body.py`, bpy 4.2 LTS): a subdivision-surface body lofted through X stations, boolean wheel arches, a greenhouse with glass and pillars, lamps, bumpers, grille, revolved tires and steel rims, a simple interior; Cycles studio render and a GLB for the phone studio page (`viewer/studio.html`, packaged by `tools/blender/package_studio.py`, checked headless by `tools/blender/smoke_studio.sh`). Every size comes from the Resolver through `tools/blender/export_params.py` (params.json keeps the provenance), so measurements replace placeholders here exactly as in the engineering build; shapes between those values (curvature, lamp and grille sizes) are visual estimates from photos E-026, not data. bpy is NOT in requirements.lock, NOT in CI and NOT in the test path: it lives in a separate Python 3.11 venv (bpy wheels need 3.11). The engineering pipeline, the CAD and every check are unchanged. Outputs stay in `build/` (D-010). | Owner feedback 2026-11-03: the block envelope is not what was asked for; a realistic, rotatable studio model on the phone is the priority. A mesh-modelling tool gives a car-like surface that the B-rep placeholder envelope cannot, while D-006 keeps it apart from engineering geometry. | ACTIVE | 2026-11-03 |
